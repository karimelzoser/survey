# Counter-UAS Visual Atlas — v3 (November 2026)

A technical, presentation-style comparison of **54 curated counter-drone system families**, organized by nine primary detection / defensive-response methods. Related Pantsir and Phalanx/Centurion versions are consolidated into family dashboards, with version-specific specifications kept distinct.

## Contents
- One interactive presentation screen per system family, with an equipment gallery, named sensor/control/response subsystems, published detection and defeat ranges, tracked-vs-engaged capacity, acquisition and action cost evidence, users/deployments, versions, comparative advantages, limitations, and direct evidence links.
- Verified videos play in-page through official publisher embeds where supported (YouTube and DVIDS). Other publisher media is linked; videos are not downloaded or rehosted without rights. Unverified videos are labeled rather than invented.
- Searchable directory, nine defence methods, full comparison table, target-class relevance filters, and a mission-specific Top 3 shortlist.\n- Each family has radar model/type, radar frequency band or an explicit **N/A / not disclosed**, drone-specific detection test reach where published, coverage, optional RF sensor band, and linked radar evidence.\n- Expanded India coverage (D4, Zen ZADS, Vajra, Indrajaal, Bhargavastra and BEL DDR), plus Norway, Brazil, Lithuania and other manufacturers.
- Local pre-existing photos and engineering diagrams in `assets/`; source credits in `MEDIA_CREDITS.md`.

## Open
Open `index.html` in a browser, or use GitHub Pages if enabled. No build step or external JS dependencies are required. Video and external publisher imagery require an internet connection.

## Interpretation
Published ranges are **not independently verified kill probabilities**. Detection distance, weapon reach, simultaneous track count, and engagement concurrency are separate metrics; launcher rounds are not simultaneous kills. Programme values are not unit prices. Unknown figures are explicitly marked undisclosed or not independently verified. Sensor-to-response diagrams are sourced functional syntheses, not unpublished OEM circuitry.

Research date: 10 November 2026. **54 is a curated flagship selection, not a count of every product worldwide.** The September 2026 industry directory lists more than 1,000 product descriptions; the atlas is not an exhaustive census. See `RESEARCH_AUDIT_NOV_2026.md` and `QA_REPORT_NOV_2026.md`.\n\nRadar instrumented range is not detection distance for a given drone; vendor Pd/Pk claims are not a common test benchmark. This website does not download or rehost third-party videos without rights. Civilian countermeasures may require legal authorization.
